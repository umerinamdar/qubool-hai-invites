# Qubool Hai Invites

Build a premium wedding invitation microsite — a single page a guest opens on their phone. A luxury product people pay for; finish matters far more than feature count. Benchmark: invitima.com.



THE AESTHETIC — "Qubool Hai"

Palette: Midnight navy and antique gold geometric.

Scene: a nikah hall at night — fine geometric star-lattice stonework, arched doorway, hanging brass lamps, deep navy marble floor, restrained and architectural, no florals.

OPENING: a folded invitation card with a geometric star pattern embossed on its cover, with the wax seal on it where the guest taps.



THE ARTWORK IS THE MOST IMPORTANT PART

Photoreal and painterly, in the register of high-end wedding stationery: real depth and shadow, genuine material weight (stone, cloth, metal), warm practical light, dense crafted detail.

NOT acceptable: flat vector, clip art, stock-photo look, plain gradients, or geometric pattern standing in for a scene. No human faces anywhere.



Four image files:

1. sealed-backdrop.jpg (1536x1024) — THE CLOSED OBJECT from the opening description, centred and filling most of the frame, symmetrical, with the scene's own setting around it (not a plain background).

2. hero-backdrop.jpg (1536x1024) — the full scene revealed once it opens. THE MIDDLE MUST BE OPEN AND QUIET: the couple's names and a date card are drawn on top of it.

3. wax-seal.png (816x816) — A RICHLY TEXTURED WAX SEAL: thick dimensional wax with soft irregular scalloped edges, glossy sheen, subtle depth. Pressed into it a RAISED GOLD ORNAMENT matching the theme (a wreath, floral ring, or geometric border) rendered as embossed metal. LEAVE THE MIDDLE CLEAR: an empty centre about 40% of the width for our engine to print initials as live text. Gold ornament YES, lettering NO. Transparent background, viewed straight down.

4. paper-texture.jpg (800x800) — subtle tileable grain in the palette.



Every image: no text, no words, no lettering, no numbers, no watermarks.



CRITICAL — THE WHOLE PAGE MUST BE AS RICH AS THE OPENING

Every section below the hero must carry the same craft: tinted paper ground not flat colour, ornamental gold dividers, carved corner flourishes, gold rules on headings, generous spacing.



BUILD RULES

Mobile first, 320px to 4K. All colours as CSS custom properties on :root, never hardcoded. Two Google Fonts: a display serif for names, a clean sans for labels. Anchor all dates to an explicit +05:30 offset. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Placeholders: "Aarav & Anaya", 14 February 2027, "The Grand Pavilion".



FINISH BY TELLING ME

1. Every colour as a hex value labelled: accent, accent-soft, tint, paper, paper-light, paper-dark, highlight, surface, scratch-foil, scratch-ink, background, foreground, muted, border.

2. The two Google Font names.

3. Where the four image files are.
